Bahūnām, among many-of many sons or disciples; emi, I go (rank); prathamah, as first, that is to say, through the foremost conduct of a disciple etc. And bahūnām, among many-many middling ones; madhyamah emi, I move (count) as a middling one, I behave through the middling conduct. "My father must have certainly spoken so out of anger without any consideration of purpose.
