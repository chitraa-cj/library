I have created these four classes based on qualities and actions.
1. Division of qualities -
Let us understand the mixture of these three qualities. Six mixtures of three qualities are possible:
1) Sattva-Rajas-Tamas - Sattva Guna is dominant, Rajas Guna is in the second place and Tamas Guna is last. Because of the dominance of Sattva Guna, a person is calm, natural, happy and simple. He has special interest in happiness, knowledge, study-teaching, renunciation, penance, spirituality, discretion, thought, research, various types of dance, music etc. arts, literature, satisfaction, acceptance of all etc. Such Sattva-dominant people are called "Brahmin" in the scriptures. A Brahmin by caste may not be Sattvic, but one who is Sattvic is definitely a Brahmin.
2) Sattva-Tamas-Rajas - Sattva Guna is dominant, Tamas Guna is in the second place and Rajas Guna is last. Where there are qualities like knowledge, discretion, penance, etc., Tamas Guna in the form of attachment cannot be possible in the second place, so this second type of mixture is impossible.
3) Rajas-Sattva-Tamas - Rajas Guna is dominant, Sattva Guna is in the second place and Tamas Guna is last. When Rajas Guna is dominant and supported by Sattva Guna, the person has the dominance of action, activity, valor, courage, ambition, politics, social welfare, protection of religion, planning, management, organization etc. are very easy. Such a person is called "Kshatriya" in the scriptures. However, it is not possible that every Kshatriya by caste is like this.
4) Rajas-Tamas-Sattva - Rajas Guna is dominant, Tamas Guna is in the second place and Sattva Guna is last. When Rajas Guna is supported by Tamas Guna, the person has the dominance of action but also has greed, selfishness, a little deceit or lies, cunning, etc. He has special interest in business, agriculture, animal husbandry, animal husbandry, etc. Such a person is called "Vaishya" in the scriptures. However, it is not necessary that every Vaishya by caste is like this.
5) Tamas-Sattva-Rajas - Tamas Guna is dominant, Sattva Guna is in the second place and Rajas Guna is last. Where Tamas Guna is dominant, Sattva Guna cannot be possible in the second place. Knowledge and laziness are mutually contradictory. Therefore, this fifth type of mixture is also impossible.
6) Tamas-Rajas-Sattva - Tamas Guna is dominant, Rajas Guna is in the second place and Sattva Guna is last. Rajas Guna is possible behind Tamas Guna, so this mixture is possible. Such a person is called "Shudra" in the scriptures. However, it is not necessary that every Shudra by caste is like this.
Thus, except for the second and fifth mixtures, four types of mixtures are found in the society - Brahmin, Kshatriya, Vaishya and Shudra. This division is found in every field of society. If there is a large industrial city, these four types of people are necessary for its operation. All those who think to advance the industry, do research are Brahmins. All those who manage and do the work of security are Kshatriyas. Vaishyas are those who keep accounts, buy raw materials and sell finished goods at high prices in the market, pay the salaries of the workers, etc. manage the financial matters. And finally, all those who do physical labor, work on machines are Shudras. Earlier there were joint families. There are also four types of family members for its operation. One or two persons are there who decide who should do what or have the responsibility of taking every decision of the family. And every member of the family respectfully accepts their decisions. These people are Brahmin by nature. And some members are engaged in the safety, health, education of all. They are Kshatriyas by nature. Some are doing the work of earning money. They are Vaishyas by nature. And some people take care of household chores, take care of children, etc. They are Shudras by nature. If we take a temple, these four types of people are also necessary in it. Brahmins worship God. Kshatriyas do the work of security and management. Vaishyas keep accounts and work to get donations, then Shudras do the cleaning of the temple and all kinds of service work. The scriptures say that one who serves honestly in the temple in this way attains heaven. There is no discrimination in the fruits of action received by them. There is no feeling of high and low in it. Also, if one of them does not do his duty properly, the whole system of industrial, family, religious institutions collapses.
